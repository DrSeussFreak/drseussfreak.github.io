CannaTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

CannaTrack, made by DrSeussFreak Inc., is a personal tracker for medical cannabis purchases, CannaCaps capsule blends and doses. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — purchases, products, potency, blends, capsule doses and notes — is health-related personal information. CannaTrack stores it:

  • On your device, so the app works offline, and
  • In your own Google Drive, in a file named cannatrack-data.json inside a “CannaTrack” folder, so your phone and computer stay in sync.

The developer does not run any server and never receives, sees or stores your data.

Google sign-in and permissions

You sign in with Google so the app can save to your Drive. CannaTrack requests:

  • Your email address and basic profile — to show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive “drive.file” access — this lets the app see and edit only the files it creates. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

CannaTrack's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It only travels between your devices and your Google Drive, over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Files you import or export

When you import a file (Markdown, CSV, XML, JSON and similar) the app reads it on your device and adds the entries to your data. Exports are saved wherever you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual entries in the app at any time; deletions sync to your other devices.
  • Signing out removes the data from that device (it stays in your Drive).
  • To delete everything, delete the “CannaTrack” folder from your Google Drive and remove CannaTrack's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

CannaTrack is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes
