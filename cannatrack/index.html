CannaTrack

Medical cannabis purchases & CannaCaps — for Android and Mac

What it does

  • Purchases — log every dispensary visit: product, strain, Indica/Sativa/Hybrid, size and the potency on the label. Search and filter by year, category and type.
  • CannaCaps blends — combine RSO, FSHO and distillate, and CannaTrack works out total THC, mg per capsule, a capsule count for your target dose and how much coconut oil to use.
  • Concentrates on hand — what you've bought minus what your blends have used; planned blends reserve it.
  • Dose tracker — tap to log capsules taken and see how many are left.
  • Import — bring in notes and lists from Markdown, CSV, TSV, XML, HTML, JSON or plain text, with a preview before anything is added.
  • Themes — dark by default, light or system, a dozen colour themes plus your own, and Material You on Android.

Private by design

You sign in with Google so your phone and Mac stay in sync. Your data is stored on your devices and in a “CannaTrack” folder in your own Google Drive — the app can only see the files it creates, and DrSeussFreak Inc. never receives your data. Read the privacy policy.

CannaTrack is a personal tracking tool for adults (18+). It doesn't sell or link to any products and isn't medical advice.
