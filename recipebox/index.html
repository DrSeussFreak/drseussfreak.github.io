Recipe Box

Your recipes, meal plans, grocery list and freezer, on your Android phone and your Mac.

  • Cookbooks and categories. Import recipes from a website link or from another recipe app's export, or write your own.
  • Scale any recipe. Ingredient amounts adjust to the servings you need.
  • Meal planner. Plan by day, week or month, then add the whole plan's ingredients to the grocery list. Each item shows which recipe it's for.
  • Grocery list. Check items off as you shop. Bought items are only removed after you confirm.
  • Freezer. Keep count of leftover portions and how long ago each was made.
  • Sync. Sign in with Google and everything syncs through a file in your own Google Drive. There's no server, and nobody else sees your data.
