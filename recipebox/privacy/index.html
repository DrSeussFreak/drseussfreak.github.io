Recipe Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Recipe Box keeps your recipes, meal plans, grocery list and freezer inventory. It runs on Android and Mac. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

Recipe Box stores what you enter or import: recipes and their photos, cookbooks and categories, planned meals, grocery items and freezer leftovers. It keeps this information:

  • On your device, so the app works offline.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you sign in. Your data is saved in a file named recipebox-data.json inside a "Recipe Box" folder, which keeps your phone and computer in sync.

You can also use the app without an account. Your data then stays on that device only.

The developer doesn't run a server. The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

Google sign-in and permissions

If you sign in with Google so the app can save to your Drive, Recipe Box requests:

  • Your email address and basic profile, to show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive "drive.file" access, which lets the app see and edit only the files it creates. It can't see any other files in your Drive.

Recipe Box's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Importing recipes

  • From a link: your device downloads that web page directly to read the recipe. The address isn't sent anywhere else.
  • From files: recipe files (for example an export from another recipe app) are read on your device. Their photos are copied into the app's storage on that device.

Voice assistants and other apps (optional)

If you set up the optional assistant hook described in the app, items are added by writing to the sync file in your own Google Drive. You deploy that hook in your own Google account, and it doesn't go through the developer.

Sharing, ads and analytics

Your data isn't sold or shared with third parties. It isn't used for advertising, and it isn't used to train AI models. Recipe Box has no ads and no analytics or tracking. Data is only sent between your devices and your Google Drive, over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

  • Delete recipes, meals, grocery items or freezer entries in the app at any time. Deletions sync to your other devices.
  • Signing out keeps a copy on that device, which comes back when you sign in again. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • To delete everything, delete the "Recipe Box" folder from your Google Drive and remove Recipe Box's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Recipe Box isn't directed at children under 13.
