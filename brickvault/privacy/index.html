BrickVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

BrickVault, made by DrSeussFreak Inc., helps you track a collection of brick building sets. The developer does not run a server and never receives, sees or stores your data.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your synced sets, wishlist, quantities, prices and price history
  • Box codes and retail barcodes you confirm in the scanner
  • Details you add: copies, condition, box, instructions, completeness, storage location, notes, price paid, ratings, wishlist priority and target price
  • Sets you add by hand or import from a file or connected account, and any seller inventory you choose to import
  • Your settings, API keys, account tokens, Brickset username, any price tracker URL and sign-in session, and any webhook address and token

This is kept in the app's private storage and is excluded from Android backups. API keys, tokens, webhook details, storage locations, notes and prices paid are also encrypted with a key held in Android Keystore. Other collection data is not separately encrypted on the device. Your Brickset and Rebrickable passwords are used only to log in and are not saved.

Services you choose to connect

  • Brickset — your API key, username and password at login, then your login token, searches, scanned barcodes and any collection or wishlist changes you make.
  • BrickEconomy — your API key, set numbers and currency, to fetch values and, if you sync it, read your BrickEconomy collection.
  • Best Buy — your API key, to list LEGO products on sale.
  • Brick Owl (optional) — your API key, set numbers and shipping country, to fetch current listing prices; to read your Brick Owl collection and wishlist if you sync them; and, if you import your seller inventory, your API key to read your store's set listings.
  • Rebrickable (optional) — your API key and set numbers, to fetch part counts; scanned barcodes, when Brickset can't identify them; and, if you import your account, your username and password once to get a token, then that token to read your sets.
  • BrickLink (optional) — your API credentials and set numbers, to fetch catalog and price-guide data; and, if you import your seller inventory, to read your store's set listings. BrickVault does not change your BrickLink store.
  • Scanning LEGO elements (optional) — only in Elements mode, and only after a one-time notice, a still photo of the part you scan is sent to Brickognize to identify it. Photos are never sent when you scan sets. Part numbers and colors are then sent to BrickLink and Rebrickable to fetch details, photos and prices. You can turn photo recognition off in Settings.
  • Rebrickable catalog (optional) — if you import Rebrickable's catalog download, it is stored only on that device to look up parts and minifigures without contacting Rebrickable.
  • Comparing sites (optional) — BrickVault reads your personal set lists from sites you connect to compare them with Brickset. These lists and your selections are stored in your encrypted sync file. Set numbers and quantities are sent to Brickset only after you review and confirm them.
  • Sending your collection (optional) — when you review and confirm a push, the set numbers and quantities you own on Brickset are sent to your Rebrickable account or your personal Brick Owl lists. Other sites use files you choose to export and import yourself.
  • Google Drive (optional) — if you turn on encrypted sync, BrickVault automatically syncs one encrypted file in your Google Drive across your devices, including in the background on Android. Your collection, personal details, API keys and tokens are encrypted on your device with a key protected by your passphrase before they are stored in your own Drive. Google and the developer cannot read them. BrickVault can only access files it created there.
  • Your own webhook (optional, off by default) — if you turn it on, BrickVault sends the set number, name, price and target to the HTTPS address you enter when a target price is reached, plus any test you send.
  • Your own price tracker (optional) — if you connect a Google Apps Script web app you control, BrickVault sends it a Google sign-in token that includes your email address, plus set numbers and prices, to get your pricing. The developer never receives this.

Those services handle this information under their own privacy policies. Product images load from the image hosts those services use, which can see your IP address, as any website can.

Web version

The optional BrickVault website runs in your browser. There is no BrickVault server. If you sign in with Google, the site opens your encrypted backup in your own Drive and decrypts it in your browser with your passphrase or recovery key. With Keep me logged in on, this browser stores a non-exportable decryption key and a sealed Drive sign-in token so it can reopen your vault until you choose Lock or Sign out. With it off, decryption keys stay in memory. Your passphrase and recovery key are never stored. Google's sign-in script runs in a separate sign-in window. If you use Rebrickable on the website, your key and set numbers go directly to Rebrickable. Set images load from Brickset and Rebrickable, which can see your IP address and which images are requested.

Camera

The camera is used only on the Scan screen. Frames are analyzed on your device to read codes and text, and are not saved or sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit library, which does this, may send Google limited diagnostic information such as device and app details and performance metrics. It does not send the camera images.

Files you export

Exports are created only when you ask and saved where you choose. They are not encrypted. CSV and XML exports can include your notes, storage locations and prices paid. BrickLink XML and Markdown exports leave those out. Anything you do with an exported file, such as sharing it or pasting it into an AI assistant, is up to you.

Store links

Store buttons open that store's website in your browser, where the store's own privacy policy applies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, BrickVault uses them for wishlist price and target-price alerts. A daily background job updates prices from the services you connected.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. The app contains no ads or analytics of its own.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall BrickVault, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete everything it stores on your device.
  • Your Brickset, BrickEconomy, Best Buy, Brick Owl, BrickLink and Rebrickable accounts are managed with those services.

Children

BrickVault is not directed at children under 13.
