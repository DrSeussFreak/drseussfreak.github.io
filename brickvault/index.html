BrickVault

Your brick set collection, values and wishlist deals — for Android

What it does

  • Your collection — sync the sets you own and want from your Brickset account, then search, sort and filter them.
  • Estimated value — see market value against retail using your own BrickEconomy API key.
  • Charts — value over time, by theme and by release year, plus your biggest gains and losses.
  • Wishlist trends — daily price records and alerts when a wishlist set drops.
  • Deals — Best Buy sales with your own free API key, and one-tap searches at the big stores.
  • Blind box scanner — read a series number or box code, pick the figure, and add it to your collection.

Private by design

BrickVault has no server and no accounts with us. Your data stays on your device and goes only to the services you connect. Read the privacy policy.
