SpiceTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

SpiceTracker, made by DrSeussFreak Inc., is a personal log of the hot sauces you try and buy. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — sauces, your heat scores and ratings, peppers, notes, purchases, stores, prices and order numbers, plus any fields you add yourself. SpiceTracker stores it:

  • On your device, so the app works offline, and
  • In your own Google Drive, in a file named hotsauce-data.json inside a “SpiceTracker” folder, so your phone and computer stay in sync. Automatic backups are saved in “SpiceTracker / Backups” in the same Drive.

The developer does not run any server and never receives, sees or stores your data.

Optional encryption

If you turn on end-to-end encryption in Settings, your data and backups are encrypted (AES-256) on your devices and in your Drive with a key that only your passphrase or recovery key can unlock. The developer can't recover your data if you lose both.

Google sign-in and permissions

You sign in with Google so the app can save to your Drive. SpiceTracker requests:

  • Your email address and basic profile — to show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive “drive.file” access — this lets the app see and edit only the files it creates. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

SpiceTracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Heatonist lookups

If you use “Check Heatonist”, the app sends the sauce's brand and name to heatonist.com to look up its heat rating. Nothing else is sent.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It only travels between your devices and your Google Drive, over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Files you import or export

When you import a file (CSV, spreadsheets, Word, Markdown, JSON and similar) the app reads it on your device and adds the entries to your data. Exports are saved wherever you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual sauces or purchases in the app at any time; deletions sync to your other devices.
  • Signing out removes the data from that device (it stays in your Drive).
  • To delete everything, delete the “SpiceTracker” folder from your Google Drive and remove SpiceTracker's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

SpiceTracker is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date.
