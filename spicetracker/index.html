SpiceTracker

Every hot sauce you've tried, rated and remembered — for Android and Mac

What it does

  • Your verdict — your own heat score (0–11), a rating out of 10, and whether you'd buy it again, plus peppers and notes.
  • Purchases — when and where you bought each bottle, the price, order number and whether it came in a hot sauce club box.
  • Search and filters — by brand, heat, rating, store, re-order and more, with sorts like hottest first or most bought.
  • Customize — edit the store and pepper lists, hide fields you don't use, or add your own fields.
  • Backups and exports — automatic backups in your Drive, and exports to CSV, JSON or Markdown. Import from spreadsheets, Word and more.
  • Optional end-to-end encryption — protect your data with a passphrase and a recovery key.

Private by design

You sign in with Google so your phone and Mac stay in sync. Your data is stored on your devices and in a “SpiceTracker” folder in your own Google Drive — the app can only see the files it creates, and DrSeussFreak Inc. never receives your data. Read the privacy policy.

Get it

SpiceTracker is in testing. A Google Play release is on the way; email drseussfreak@gmail.com if you'd like to join the test.
