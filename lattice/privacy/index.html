Lattice Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Lattice, made by DrSeussFreak Inc., is a Lines & Boxes strategy game. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your device

AI matches, daily puzzles and Pass & Play run on your device and work offline. Your stats, streaks, match history and chosen nickname are stored only on your device. Lattice has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Online rooms

Online rooms are optional. If you create or join one, the app connects to the game server over an encrypted connection and sends:

  • the preset nickname you picked,
  • a random player ID created by the app, which is not linked to your identity,
  • the room code, your moves, and any preset emoji reactions.

The other player in your room sees your nickname, moves and reactions. There is no free-text chat. The game server keeps room information in temporary memory only, and removes a room after everyone has left and it has been idle for about 30 minutes.

As with any internet service, the server's hosting provider (Google Cloud) may briefly process technical details such as your IP address to deliver the connection and keep the service secure.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Purchases

Purchases and Google Play Pass access are handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Lattice, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Online room information is removed automatically as described above.

Children

Lattice is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date.
