StudCraft

Turn any picture into a buildable brick mosaic, on the web, Android and Mac

What it does

  • Picture to mosaic — start with a photo, then choose the size, palette, build style and backing.
  • Create an image with AI — describe what you want and StudCraft makes a picture ready to turn into a mosaic.
  • Your choice of AI — turn on Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or any mix using your own API keys, and compare their answers.
  • Mosaic editor — choose real brick colors, paint and fill studs, replace colors, and tweak the image.
  • Pro exports — save PNG posters and stud maps, PDF instructions, and BrickLink Studio files.
  • Build checks — review color matches and structural connections before you buy parts.
  • Parts and pricing — get a full parts list, cost estimates and marketplace-ready exports.
  • Step-by-step guide — follow the build section by section and keep your place.

You stay in control

You can use StudCraft without an account. API keys you add are stored securely on your device, and AI requests go only to the providers you set up. Read the privacy policy.

Get it

Open StudCraft in your browser. No account or install needed.

The Android and Mac apps are in testing. Email drseussfreak@gmail.com to try them.
