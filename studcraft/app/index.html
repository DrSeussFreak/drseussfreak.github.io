Image and summary

Your AI, your account

AI is optional. When you press Ask or Create, your prompt and a short mosaic summary go directly from this browser to each AI you enabled, and usage bills to that provider account. Your images are not sent. StudCraft has no server and never sees your keys.

AI keys

Remember on this device (optional)

Keys are held in memory and disappear when you close this tab. To keep them, encrypt them with a passphrase of at least 10 characters. The passphrase is never stored and cannot be recovered.

Encrypted settings backup

Download your AI keys, models, and theme in one file locked with a passphrase of at least 12 characters, then keep it in your Google Drive folder. It also opens in StudCraft for Android and Mac. The passphrase is never stored. If you lose it, this backup cannot be recovered.

    Cloud Library

    Sign in with Google to keep mosaic settings in your account and open them on Android or here. Everything else in StudCraft works without an account.

    Save current mosaic

    Saves the title, notes, build settings, and totals to your account. Images, AI keys, and settings backups are never uploaded.

    Your saved mosaics

    Saved mosaics do not include an image or manual stud edits. Samples can be rebuilt. For your own images, the settings are applied to the image open now. Brightness, contrast, and saturation are not saved.
